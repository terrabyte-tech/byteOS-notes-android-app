# byteOS-notes-android-app

Remember the good ol' days of tech where mega-corporations weren't always spying on you and AI wasn't removing your personality with every draft edit? Or when you didn't need to upgrade your phone every two years to keep your apps running as they should? Us too. Enter byteOS - a simple suite of everyday apps that instill nostalgia for those on newer devices and bring modern-yet-minimal utilities to those on legacy hardware. Why upgrade when you can downgrade?

## Features

By design, byteOS: Notes is feature-minimal. Creating, editing, and saving notes are first and foremost, but quality-of-life additions like auto-saving, draft management, and text widgets (coming soon) keep modern utilities in your court.

## Contribute
